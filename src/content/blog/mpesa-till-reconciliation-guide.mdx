For many Kenyan shops, more money now comes in through M-Pesa than through the cash drawer. That's convenient
for customers, but it creates a daily job that many owners struggle with: making sure every M-Pesa payment
matches a sale, and every sale that was "paid by M-Pesa" really was.

This guide sets out a simple routine for reconciling your till every day, and the habits that stop small
mismatches from turning into real losses.

## Why M-Pesa and sales don't match

Before fixing the problem, it helps to know where differences come from. The most common causes are:

- **Payments confirmed by SMS.** A cashier sees a message on their phone — or a customer shows one — and
  releases the goods. Messages can be misread, belong to a different payment, or be fake.
- **Wrong amounts.** The customer sends KES 1,500 for a KES 1,550 sale, and nobody notices.
- **Payments with no sale.** Money arrives, but the sale was never recorded — or was recorded as cash.
- **Sales with no payment.** A sale is marked "M-Pesa" but the money never came in.
- **Reversals.** A payment is reversed after the goods have left.
- **Timing.** Late-evening payments fall on the wrong side of your closing time.

Each one is small on its own. Together, over a month, they add up.

## The daily routine

Set aside ten minutes at close of business — every day, not once a month. The longer you wait, the harder it is
to remember what happened.

### 1. Close every till and shift

Each cashier should close their own shift before going home, recording how much cash they hold. Separate shifts
make it possible to trace a difference to a person and a time, instead of to "the shop".

### 2. Get your M-Pesa statement for the day

Download or view the statement for your till or paybill for the same period as your sales report — from your
opening time to your closing time.

### 3. Compare totals first

Compare the total M-Pesa received with the total of sales marked as M-Pesa. If they agree, you are probably
done. If they don't, the difference tells you how much you are looking for.

### 4. Match payment by payment

Work through the differences using:

- **amount** — look for the exact figure, then for amounts that are close;
- **time** — payments usually land within a minute or two of the sale; and
- **the customer's name or number** on the statement.

Mark each payment as matched as you go. What's left are the exceptions.

### 5. Deal with every exception the same day

For each unmatched item, decide what happened and record it:

- a payment with no sale → find the sale, or ask the cashier on duty;
- a sale with no payment → check for a wrong till number, a failed payment, or a fake message;
- a reversal → record it against the original sale.

Write down what you found. Patterns — the same cashier, the same time of day — are worth acting on.

## Habits that prevent problems

- **Never release goods on the strength of an SMS alone.** Confirm the payment on the till's own record or in
  your M-Pesa business account.
- **Use one till number per shop**, displayed clearly at the counter, so customers don't pay the wrong one.
- **Give each cashier their own login**, so every sale and every payment is linked to a person.
- **Reconcile daily.** A difference found the same evening is easy to explain; one found at month-end usually
  isn't.
